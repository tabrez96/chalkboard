# Chalkboard

## The problem
Building a course from scratch takes hours: you collect material, shape it into chapters and fit every lesson to the length of your class. AI lesson planners write quickly, but you can't tell where their sentences came from or whether your trusted sources actually say that.

## How it helps
- You choose the sources: your own files and pages from the websites you trust.
- It turns them into a course overview, then chapters, then lesson plans sized to your class length.
- Every sentence shows which source it comes from, and sentences the source doesn't back are marked for you to check.
- Nothing changes without your approval, and it runs on your own computers.

## Who it's for
Teachers who plan their own courses and want every lesson grounded in material they already trust.
