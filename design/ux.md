# Chalkboard: UX spec

## Users and primary jobs
One teacher per account, building and teaching courses from sources they trust.
1. Collect trusted sources for a subject: uploads, and pages saved from the websites the subject is allowed to use.
2. Turn them into a course step by step: the course overview (the map), then each chapter, then class-length lesson plans, approving each step and seeing the source of every sentence.
3. Teach today's lesson from its plan, with an assistant that answers from the sources or, when asked, from the web, and always says which.

## Information architecture
- S01 Sign in
- S09 Teacher guide: a one-page guide to setup and the daily workflow, readable without signing in, linked from S01 and S08, printable
- S02 Subjects (all subjects, coming-up classes, create a subject or edit its settings: class length, curriculum board, timetable, term, websites)
- Inside a subject, left sidebar: subject switcher at the top (no section heading), then S06 Today, S04 Course overview, S05 Chapters, S03 Sources, S07 Students
- No subject chosen (S02, and S08 when reached from S02): the switcher reads "Choose a subject" with no subject selected, and the subject pages are hidden until one is chosen. S08 reached from a subject keeps that subject and its pages.
- Collapsible sidebar on every screen that has one: a toggle at the top (beside the logo) collapses it to a 68px icon rail: logo mark, subject initial (or a grid icon when none is chosen), one icon per page, footer icons (All subjects, Settings, theme, Ollama status, avatar). Each icon has an `aria-label` and a tooltip; hover or keyboard focus shows the label. The choice is remembered per account. Collapsed: S04 `states/sidebar-collapsed.html`.
- Sidebar footer: All subjects (S02), Settings (S08), Light/Dark toggle, Ollama connection status (links to S08), the teacher's name.

## Key flows
**First run**: S01 → S02 empty, create a subject with class length, curriculum board (CBSE, ICSE, a named state board, IB, or Other or none), class days and times, and the term's first and last day (optional, can be added later), an optional Syllabus PDF beside the board (upload, or one of the subject's sources), and where sources come from (Uploads only, or External websites with one or more allowed sites ticked) → S03 the syllabus sits pinned at the top of the sources table (or add it here with Add syllabus PDF / Choose from sources), then files or saved pages → wait for "Ready".
**Timetable**: S02 subject form → days, times and term give the number of classes and the total teaching time (the course budget). Lessons fill the class days in order; Next class, Coming up (S02) and Today (S06) take their dates from it. No timetable: S02 card and S06 ask for class times, S04 shows hours with no budget. Term too short: S02 card chip and S04 over-budget alert say what won't fit and link to subject settings.
**Syllabus**: with a board chosen, the syllabus is the overview's spine: its units, in its order, become the chapters, and the other sources supply the content. Each chapter cites its unit ("Syllabus · Unit N"); S04's coverage check flags units no chapter covers (Ask to add it, or Not this term). The syllabus is a source: one per subject, pinned at the top of S03's table with a Syllabus chip and no source number. It is set from the S02 form (Syllabus PDF), or on S03 with the row action Use as syllabus; setting another replaces it, and the old one becomes a normal source. No units found: the teacher types them (S03 Type the units). No syllabus yet: S03 shows a line above the table (Add syllabus PDF, Choose from sources) and S04 says it is planning from sources alone with no coverage check. Other or none: S04 works from the sources alone.
**Overview (the map)**: S04 Generate (chapters follow the syllabus units when there is one; the budget is spread across chapters) → generating (find passages, write, check every sentence) → read the draft; each sentence carries citation numbers, one per source it uses → open a number to see the passage, or a unit chip to see the syllabus unit → check Coverage → "Check this" sentences explain the mismatch → chapter hours are read-only estimates fitted to the term budget, with the total against it → Request changes (new version, including any change to hours) or Approve.
**Chapter (the content)**: S05 the next chapter's topic is taken from the approved overview (rename or add notes if wanted) → Write chapter draft → review as on S04 → Approve and split into classes (3 h at 60 min = 3 classes) → each class is a lesson plan with timings, activities and citations.
**Teach**: S06 shows today's lesson plan and its sources. After the class, the Trivia for class card offers 3 to 5 questions with answers and citations; the teacher edits one or asks for a new one. The assistant answers from "Your sources" by default; switching to "Sources + web" lets it search the web, and those answers are labelled "From the web" with their links. If the sources don't cover a question, the answer says "Not in your sources".
**Teacher guide**: S09 condenses the flows above for a teacher: first-time setup (S01, S08, S02 with the board, S03 with the syllabus, S04, S05, S07), a normal day (S06 before, during and after class) and the chapter loop (S05).
**Server down**: any model action shows "Can't reach the Ollama server" → Check connection → S08 Test connection. Sources and approved plans stay readable.
**Websites**: S08 holds the allowed list (and open mode); each subject chooses one of two options in its form on S02: **Uploads only**, or **External websites**, which shows a checklist of the allowed sites where one or more are ticked (at least one is required). The server only saves pages from the ticked sites. S03 names the ticked sites in one line and shows the link field and Save page only when a site is ticked.
**Theme, back up, delete**: sidebar toggle or S08.

## Design system
- **Look**: modern, flat and calm. Minimal: one job per screen, short labels instead of explanations, row actions as icons, no cards that only explain. Light surfaces with soft shadows, generous spacing, rounded corners, a single green accent; chalk yellow is the brand spark (logo, focus ring).
- **Colours, light theme**: page `#F5F7F6`, surface `#FFFFFF`, surface 2 `#EEF2F0`, line `#E1E6E4`, ink `#111917`, muted `#56625E`, primary `#0E7A64` (text on it `#FFFFFF`), primary soft `#E2F3EE` with text `#0A6452`, chalk yellow `#F2B531` (focus ring, logo; never text on light), check/flag `#8A3A0C` on `#FFF0E0` with line `#E3843A`, ok `#17603A` on `#E0F4E8`, draft `#6B5200` on `#FFF3C4`, web `#3A3EA6` on `#ECEEFF`, danger `#B3261E`.
- **Colours, dark theme**: page `#0D1412`, surface `#141D1A`, surface 2 `#1B2622`, line `#25312D`, ink `#E7EDEB`, muted `#9AA7A2`, primary `#2BB594` (text on it `#04211A`), primary soft `#163A31` with text `#74D9BE`, flag `#FFC797` on `#3A2312`, ok `#8FE0AE` on `#13301F`, web `#BFC2FF` on `#22254A`, danger `#FF8A80`. Theme: Light, Dark or Match system; stored per account.
- **Subject colours**: Physics `#0E7A64`, Mathematics `#3A57C9`, Chemistry `#B4492B` (from a fixed set of six); identification only.
- **Type**: headings Raleway (bold, 700–800); body and UI Inter, 15px/1.5. Raleway's default old-style numerals make times, tables and citation numbers uneven, so it stays on headings. Both are open-licence fonts bundled with the app (no font service). Scale: 26 page title, 18 card title, 15 body, 13 small, 12 chips and hints.
- **Spacing**: 4px base; cards 18–20px padding, 14px gap; radius 14 cards, 9 buttons and inputs, pill chips.
- **List rows**: at most two items on a line. Labels and chips (chapter number, syllabus unit, status) go in a small line above the title; numbers sit on the right with their unit underneath ("3 h" over "3 classes · Estimate"). Text never wraps beside a chip.
- **Components**: sidebar with subject switcher and collapse toggle (icon rail when collapsed, labels as tooltips); card; syllabus row (S03, pinned, primary soft fill with square ends and a straight 3px primary bar the full height of its left edge, Syllabus chip); accent edges (left rules) are always straight bars on square-cornered elements, never curved on rounded ones; no-syllabus line (S03, dashed amber) and "Syllabus · Unit N" chip (S04, primary soft, opens the unit text); coverage list (unit, chapter chip or amber "Not covered"); primary / secondary / ghost / danger buttons; icon buttons for row actions (trash to delete or remove, bookmark for Use as syllabus, pencil to edit, arrows for previous/next), each with an `aria-label` and tooltip; site checklist (checkboxes in a labelled fieldset, S02); request box (multi-line text area with "This writes version N" and the buttons on the line below); segmented control; status chips; citation number (source number, small rounded square; amber with outline when the passage doesn't back the sentence); "Check this" chip plus highlighted sentence; banners (error, warning, info); passage panel with a yellow quote rule; source list; chat bubbles with "From your sources" (green), "Not in your sources" (dashed amber) and "From the web" (indigo, with links); checklist; switch.
- **Rules shown in the UI**: the syllabus decides chapters and order, but sentences cite teaching sources, never the syllabus; chapter hours are read-only and labelled "Estimate", with the total against the term budget; they change only through Request changes; every sentence carries at least one citation number; changes always say "This writes version N"; student-name protection is stated where names are typed (S06, S07); web answers are always labelled.
- **Sample data**: teacher Aamina; subjects Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry.
- **Tone of voice**: plain, calm, teacher-to-teacher. Say what happened and what to do next. No AI hype. Name the model server plainly ("Ollama server").
- **Logo**: a pair of chalk-yellow citation brackets around a white chalk stroke, on a deep board-green rounded tile (`#0F2B25`). It reads as a small blackboard whose writing is held in a citation: "what's on the board has a source". `mark.svg` is the app icon, favicon and sidebar mark (the tile makes it work on light and dark); `logo.svg` is the mark with "Chalkboard" in Raleway ExtraBold, board green, for light backgrounds such as S01's form side. On dark backgrounds use the mark with white text. Don't recolour the brackets or place the logo on yellow.

## Accessibility
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1 in both themes (muted on page, white on primary, chip text on chip backgrounds). Yellow is never text on light.
- Visible 3px yellow focus ring on every control; tab order: sidebar, then main content top to bottom, then right column.
- "Check this", "Not in your sources" and "From the web" never rely on colour alone: they carry text chips.
- Citation numbers are links labelled "Citation, source N"; the passage panel is reachable by keyboard and returns focus to the number.
- The sidebar toggle is a button with `aria-expanded` and the label "Collapse sidebar" / "Expand sidebar"; in the icon rail, every link keeps its name (`aria-label`) and shows it as a tooltip on hover and focus.
- Theme toggle and scope switch are button groups with `aria-pressed`; switches use `role="switch"`.
- Progress and errors use `role="status"` / `role="alert"`. All inputs have labels.

## Open questions
- Reading units from a board's syllabus PDF: layouts vary by board and year, and scanned PDFs have no text. When no units are found, the teacher types them (S03). How units are detected is an architecture choice.
- Web search for the assistant needs a search service the app can reach; a self-hosted one keeps to the local-only rule as far as possible. Which one is an architecture choice.
- Holidays and skipped dates: not in the MVP; the term is every chosen weekday between its first and last day.
- Language of the material: the UI is English; RTL or other scripts are untested.
- Generation time targets once the server hardware is known; long runs may need a notification when done.
